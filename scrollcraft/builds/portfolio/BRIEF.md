## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Precise, trustworthy, detail-obsessed, not boring. A printed test report. White paper, black ink, one green PASS stamp.
2. **Journey (owner's order, content unchanged).** Claim, evidence, person, tools, experience, projects as test cases, AI testing, process, testimonials, credentials, result, contact.
3. **Energy curve.** Calm and exact at the start, steady through the body, quietest right before the Result, then one loud moment, then resolved.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** See the feeling curve and peak below.
5. **The thing no other site does.** The page audits itself while you read it, with real browser measurements, and signs the report at the bottom.
6. **Range.** Chaptered editorial. Disciplined, typographic, ruled. Not maximalist, not minimal-luxury.
7. **World.** Distinct chapters (hard cuts between grounds), not one continuous world.
8. **Assets.** Owner's own: portrait photo, "Keep the Earth Green" banner, resume PDF. No generated media, no video.

## Grammar

Chaptered editorial, as a test report. Chapters are numbered test cases (TC-nn). A
margin rail (desktop) or status strip (mobile) is the folio and the navigation.
Hard cuts between grounds. The close is a sign-off block, not a button island.

## Signature move: the self-audit

A run line sweeps down the viewport as the visitor scrolls. Each chapter stamp goes
UNVERIFIED, then RUNNING, then PASSED n/n (or FAILED, honestly). The checks are real
and computed in the visitor's browser on that chapter: image alt, heading structure,
link names, computed text contrast, tap-target size, horizontal overflow, document
language, title, viewport zoom, single h1, main landmark. The Result chapter prints
real measured LCP and CLS (only when the browser reports them), the tally of checks,
and uncaught runtime errors. It states plainly that this is not a full audit.

## The peak

Chapter 10, Result. The one dark intertitle on the page and the tallest span.
Visitor's sentence to a friend: **"The page ran a real audit on itself while I
scrolled, then printed the result and signed it at the bottom."**

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where the portfolio of a QA engineer tests itself in front of you.

## Authored silence

Chapter 09 (Credentials and the banner) is deliberately still: no counting, no sweep
events other than its own stamp. It exists so the Result has something to cut from.

## Reduced motion and no-motion states

No sweep, no running state, no sticky print. Every stamp shows its final measured
state after fonts settle; the Result is fully printed. Content is identical.
